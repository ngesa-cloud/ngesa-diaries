## 1. Character & Voice Profile Specification

| Attribute | Specification |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Demographic** | Kenyan male, late 30s to early 40s. |
| **Accent / Dialect** | Neutral East African accent (Nairobi Standard English). Neither British received pronunciation nor Americanized cadence. Authentic, educated, conversational Kenyan cadence. |
| **Tone & Demeanor** | **Measured, low-warmth, serious.** The voice of an investigative journalist who has seen too much and is choosing every word carefully. Never cheerful, never performative, never sensational. |
| **Subtext / Texture** | **Underlying tension.** Eerie stillness. Think *BBC Africa Eye* meets Jack Rhysider (*Darknet Diaries*) meets true-crime noir. |
| **Pace** | **~130–145 words per minute.** Deliberate, unhurried cadence with purposeful dramatic pauses before revealing pivotal facts. |
| **Pitch & Dynamics** | Steady volume, grounded baritone / lower register. **Strictly no rising inflection at sentence endings** (avoid uptalk; end sentences with downward finality). |
| **Microphone Distance** | Close proximity (~4–6 inches) with pop filter; intimate proximity effect without plosives or excessive mouth clicks. |

## 2. Sample Audition & Test Lines (30-Second Preview Scripts)

Please record the following lines in a single continuous take, maintaining the deliberate 135 wpm investigative cadence.

### Sample A: The Nocturnal Rural Hook (Episode 01)
> *"It’s three-fifteen in the morning along the shores of Kendu Bay. [0.6s pause] The crickets suddenly stop chirping. Then comes the sound every child in western Kenya was warned about: a violent slap of wet mud against corrugated iron sheets. [0.8s pause] You stay in bed. You do not light a match. ### Sample B: The Highway Corridor Mystery (Episode 06)

When Hassan looked in his rearview mirror at eighty kilometers an hour, the passenger seat was empty. [0.7s pause] The seatbelt, however, was still clicked shut."*

### Sample C: Investigative Declassification (Editorial Intro)

## 4. Technical Audio Delivery Specifications

* **Sampling Rate:** 48.0 kHz / 24-bit PCM WAV (uncompressed raw delivery).
* **Target Loudness:** Standardized to **-16.0 LUFS** (integrated loudness) with maximum True Peak at **-1.5 dBFS**.
* **Room Acoustics:** Fully treated dry studio booth (no room reverb, comb filtering, or slapback). Noise floor `< -60 dBFS`.
* **Delivery Artifacts:** Raw WAV stems, cleaned edit with breath attenuation (natural pauses preserved), and accompanying metadata manifest.
